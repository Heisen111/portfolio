# Aadi — Portfolio

> A cinematic portfolio built as a visual story.

A one-page portfolio for **Devarshi Dave (Aadi)** — a developer focused on smart contract systems, blockchain, and autonomous AI agents.

The portfolio follows a Japanese-inspired cinematic narrative:

**ARRIVAL → THE PATH → THE CRAFT → THE WORK → THE HUMAN → THE NEXT JOURNEY**

The goal isn't to make a website that _looks_ Japanese or samurai-inspired.

It should feel like a film.

---

## Experience

The portfolio is structured as a continuous visual journey rather than a traditional collection of pages.

### ARRIVAL

The opening sequence establishes the atmosphere through cinematic motion, typography, sound, and minimal interaction.

### THE PATH

A visual introduction to the direction and journey behind the work.

### THE CRAFT

A technical overview of the systems and tools used to build.

### THE WORK

Selected projects and engineering work.

### THE HUMAN

A quieter section exploring the person behind the technical work, including photography and personal interests.

### THE NEXT JOURNEY

The closing chapter — ending the experience with a quiet editorial Q&A and a sense of continuation beyond the site.

---
